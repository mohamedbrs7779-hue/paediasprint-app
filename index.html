The initial question bank covers Foundation of Practice (FOP), Theory and Science (TAS), and Applied Knowledge in Practice (AKP). Check the published question count before purchasing; the first release is a limited starter collection.

Can I try questions without paying?+

Yes. You can explore a small selection of independently written best-of-five questions without a credit card.
